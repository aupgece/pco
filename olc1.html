1Submitting a record for the first time

Open "Record a class" (the first tab).

1

Pick your name, the date, and the timetable day

Type a few letters of your name and click it from the list, then pick the actual calendar date the class happened on. Leave the third box on "Same weekday as the date above" unless your class ran on a different day's timetable.

2

Your classes for that day appear automatically

One card shows up per class on your timetable for that day, with the subject, section and time already filled in — you don't type those.

3

Fill in each class card

For every class, answer whether it was held on the scheduled time, then fill in attendance, GCR upload status, and the class link.

"Students absent" fills itself in as enrolled − present — you only need to overwrite it if it's wrong.
4

Submit the class

Click the button at the bottom of each card. Nothing is saved until you press it — nothing is auto-saved in the background. Do this for every class card on the page — each one submits separately.

2Editing a record you already submitted

You can correct a submitted record any time — there's no lock, and no need to contact anyone.

1

Reopen the same name and date

Go back to "Record a class," select the same name and the exact same date you submitted for. Your previous answers load straight back into the cards.

2

Change whatever needs correcting

Edit any field directly — attendance numbers, the held yes/no answer, the link, remarks — exactly like the first time.

3

Submit again to save the correction

The button now reads "Update this class" instead of "Submit this class." Click it to save your changes. Same rule as before: it only saves once you click the button.

Admin-only features (reports, timetable uploads, deleting entries) live under the separate "Admin" tab and aren't covered here.

Non-compliance report — faculty who did not initiate online classes

Scans the timetable against submitted records over a date range and lists every scheduled class that has either no record at all ("No record submitted" — the faculty member never logged it) or a record marked "Held on scheduled time: No". Narrow it to a specific timetable time slot to check who did not conduct/submit for that particular period. Use this to follow up with faculty who did not initiate/effect their online classes.
